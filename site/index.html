Connect it once.

There is one address, and it is the whole of the setup. You paste it once, in your assistant's settings, and sign in with Google. Nothing is installed and nothing is configured afterwards.

https://euroleague-analytics-mcp.fly.dev/mcp

You cannot ask the assistant to connect itself — pasting this page or the address into a chat does nothing. It takes one visit to settings. Pick yours:

Verified — connected and answering, on the free plan too

  1. Open Customize, then Connectors.
  2. Click +, choose Add custom connector, paste the address above, click Add.
  3. Sign in with Google when Claude opens the sign-in window.
  4. In a chat, open + → Connectors and switch it on. Seventeen tools appear under its name.

Works in Claude on the web, on desktop and on the phone. In Claude Code instead:

claude mcp add --transport http euroleague https://euroleague-analytics-mcp.fly.dev/mcp

Paid plans only, and in review for the ChatGPT app directory since 2 September 2026

ChatGPT adds custom connectors on Plus, Pro and Business, on the web, with Developer mode switched on. When the directory listing is approved you will add it by name; until then, this is the way.

  1. Open Settings → Security and login and switch on Developer mode.
  2. Go to Settings → Plugins (this section was called Connectors until mid-2026), then +.
  3. Name it European Basketball Analytics, paste the address above under Connection, and pick OAuth.
  4. Sign in with Google when ChatGPT opens the sign-in window, then turn the connector on in a chat.

Verified with Gemini Spark — tag with @ in any chat

Custom connectors work in Gemini Spark (Google AI Pro or Ultra, personal account, 18+). The free consumer Gemini app does not support custom connectors yet.

  1. Open Settings & help → Connected apps → Custom apps for Spark → Add a custom app.
  2. Name it EuroLeague Analytics, paste the address above, and sign in with Google.
  3. In any chat, tag @EuroLeague Analytics and ask your question in plain language.

In the terminal with Google Antigravity CLI (agy) instead:

agy mcp add euroleague https://euroleague-analytics-mcp.fly.dev/mcp

If you are on the free plan, the shortest path is the Claude tab: it takes one custom connector for free, and it is four clicks.

Ask it something hard.

The hard part is the reading: asking the next question, rejecting a weak answer, and naming what was left out.

Three real sessions against the running server. Every figure and caveat below came back from it.

It asks the next question for you.

One threshold is an opinion. A useful answer moves the threshold and shows you what happens, then tells you where the number stops being worth quoting.

It refuses the flashy answer.

Every lineup response carries the possessions behind it and a warning that small samples are noise. An assistant that reads its own tool output acts on that: the +76 five on 54 possessions is named and set aside, and the one it would repeat out loud is the one with real minutes.

It tells you what it left out.

Games that fail a validation invariant are held back from every answer, and the response says how many and why. Nothing is deleted and you can ask for them — but you are told before you quote a number, not after.
